      Problem
      Item analysis after every test takes hours of tallying, so remediation is often guessed.
      Approach
      Encode responses once; formulas and macros rank competencies and learners and draft the plan.
      Outcome
      Analysis, remediation plan and DepEd-style reports from one workbook, for up to 60 items.

      • Printable DepEd-style reports with signatories

      File 08 · DALIguro module

      QR assessment: from test paper to grade book.

      Problem
      Scores from paper tests are retyped into the grade book one learner at a time.
      Approach
      Give each learner a QR code, encode scores in batches with validation, sync to grading.
      Outcome
      QR codes, printable exams and batch encoding work; camera scanning and AI reading are still in development.

      The QR encoding module of DALIguro. Teachers stop retyping test scores; the scores move into grading on their own.

      • Learner QR codes and printable exams
      • Fast and batch score encoding with validation
      • Each request stays inside the signed-in teacher's own school and assessment
